## Role

## Project Context
Our organization is migrating away from CA Broadcom mainframe (z/OS) products to replacement products, across ~21-22 applications. This specific task covers one product pair within that migration:

- **Current product:** CA Fast Load for DB2 z/OS and CA Fast Unload for DB2 z/OS
- **Replacement product:** IBM DB2 Management Pack Solution Pack for z/OS
- **Scope owner for this task:** Application team — responsible for impact assessment and application-side changes only. Installation/uninstallation of the new product is owned by a separate infrastructure team and is out of scope here.
- **Component types potentially affected:** JCL, PROCs, CTRLCARDS invoking Fast Load/Fast Unload utilities (or DBUTILITY/DDUPDATE-related steps where relevant), and any COBOL/CICS/Datacom programs that reference these jobs or their outputs.
